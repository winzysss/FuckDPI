<p><i>FuckDPI</i> is an open-source Android app, built for educational purposes, that bypasses DPI-based blocking by running a local desync engine on your device and routing all traffic through it — no remote server, no accounts.</p>
<p>It uses Android's VpnService to redirect traffic, but the tunnel runs entirely inside your device. It does not encrypt traffic or hide your IP address.</p>
<p><b>Smart Mode</b><br>One tap is all it takes: on first connect, the app quickly tests curated strategies against real sites and picks the best one automatically.</p>
<p>
<b>Features</b><br>
* Smart Mode — automatic strategy discovery for your network<br>
* One-tap connect with a clean, simple main screen<br>
* VPN and Proxy modes (standalone SOCKS5)<br>
* Per-app filtering (split tunneling)<br>
* Autostart on device boot<br>
* In-app updates from GitHub Releases<br>
* Turkish, English, Russian, Kazakh, Vietnamese<br>
* Android TV/BOX support + Quick Settings tile<br>
